How To
Every Show, In Order
1
Set the event — Tap "Set / Edit Event" on the Current Event page. Enter name, date, location, and price if known. Leave price blank if the venue is setting it.
2
Count In — Before the show, enter starting inventory per size. Tap Save Count In.
3
Count Out — After the show, enter what's left per size. Tap Save Count Out. Sold calculates automatically.
4
Add Notes — Log anything worth remembering: venue rep name, shortages, issues.
5
Close the Event — When numbers look right, tap Close Event. This locks the record. Double-check first — it can't be undone.
6
Print or Email — Tap Print / Email to generate a paper form or open a pre-filled email summary.

📝Notes are permanent. Log problems before you forget.
